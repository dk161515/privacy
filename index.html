Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and shares information when you install and use the Developer QR Scanner & Generator mobile app.

01

Data Collection & Processing

Our application operates entirely offline to perform its core functions. We do not host or operate external backend servers to process your documents, custom templates, or QR codes. All code generation, image decodings, and favorites caching occur locally on your physical device.

02

Permissions Required

To deliver our scan features, the application requires authorization for the following hardware components:

  • Camera: Used to stream video frames locally in the viewbox to scan and decode barcodes and QR codes. Camera feeds are never recorded or transmitted off-device.
  • Photo Library / Storage: Used to allow you to import images from your gallery to scan and generate codes from them.
03

Monetization & Advertisements

Free plans display advertisements using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). The SDK collects specific identifiers (e.g., Ad ID, device info, IP address) under Google’s Privacy Policy rules to serve, target, and optimize ads. Purchases of premium plans instantly deactivate all ad integrations.

04

Billing & Payments

All in-app purchases and lifetime plan upgrades are processed securely via Google Play Billing. We do not collect, view, or store your credit card details or payment credentials. Entitlements are bound directly to your Google Account.

05

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that their child has provided us with information, they should contact us immediately, and we will take necessary steps to remove such details.

06

Contact Information

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support email: developer.qrscannergenerator@gmail.com
